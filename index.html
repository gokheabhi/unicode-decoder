// byte reader

Decode raw bytes to text by abhishek

Paste bytes exactly as stored or dumped from MySQL, PostgreSQL, or anywhere else — hex, \x/\u escapes, comma-separated decimals, or base64. This reads every common text encoding (UTF-8, UTF-16 LE/BE, UTF-32 LE/BE, UCS-2 LE/BE, Latin-1, Windows-1252) and ranks the most likely result. There's no encryption here — character encodings aren't encryption, so anything can be read without a key.

Results will appear here, best match first.
Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
